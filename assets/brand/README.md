# Postino brand assets

Original artwork by the Postino contributors, licensed like the code (MIT OR Apache-2.0).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `postino-mark.svg` | Full colour on ivory/white/photo with contrast: forest tile, tilted ivory envelope, gold cockade |
| `postino-mark-inverse.svg` | On forest or dark surfaces: ivory tile, forest envelope, gold cockade |
| `postino-mark-mono.svg` | One-colour contexts: forest strokes on a transparent background |
| `proposta-logo.svg` | The original sketch; kept for reference, not for production |

## Rules

- Minimum size: 24 px for the full mark; 16 px only for the mono version or when the cockade may
  collapse to a gold point.
- Clear space: at least 25% of the tile on every side.
- The envelope is drawn at 7° clockwise; do not rotate the tile or the artwork further, and do not
  stretch, outline, recolour or add effects.
- The cockade is a seal, not a star. The star belongs to the favourite control and must not be used
  as the brand.
- On a busy photograph, use the full-colour tile; it has its own forest background.
- The wordmark is lowercase **postino**. The dot of the `i` is gold; the rest stays forest or ivory.

Palette: forest `#173D32`, ivory `#F5EBD6`, gold `#E4B96A`. Gold on ivory is decorative only.
